BEFORE YOU JOIN

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Can beginners join the club?

Yes. You do not need any previous experience. Our community welcomes students who want to learn the fundamentals.

When are the training sessions?

Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 18:00. Check the Training page for the session focus.

How do I become a member?

Create a demo account on the Join page. Your member profile and saved sessions stay on this browser.

Is this an online registration service?

This is a student website project. Registration and messages are simulated locally and do not notify the club.
